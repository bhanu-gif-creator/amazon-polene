P4 · Product Communication

On-Page eCommerce Strategy — Shopify Plus

We recommend Shopify Plus to create a premium online shopping experience that reflects Polène's identity while helping customers understand the size, functionality, materials, colours and differences between bag models before purchasing.

1. High-Quality Product ImagesFront, back, side, interior, leather close-ups, strap details and lifestyle images.
2. 360° Product View & VideoLet customers rotate bags and see how each model looks when worn.
3. “What Fits Inside?”Show realistic capacity using a phone, wallet, keys, sunglasses, card holder and small cosmetics.
4. Product Comparison ToolCompare bag styles, capacity and recommended use to simplify the purchase decision.
5. Customer ReviewsRatings, written reviews and customer photographs to increase trust and reassurance.
6. Personalized RecommendationsYou May Also Like, Similar Styles, Complete the Look and Recently Viewed.
7. International LocalizationAdapt language, currency, local pricing, delivery and returns for France, UK, USA and other markets.
8. Mobile-First DesignFast, easy access to images, colours, product information and the Add to Bag button on smartphones.
9. Clear Delivery & ReturnsShow estimated delivery dates and return conditions directly on product pages.
10. AnalyticsTrack product views, add-to-cart, conversion, checkout abandonment, AOV, most-viewed bags and comparison usage.

Proposed Customer Journey

Final Recommendation

Shopify Plus + 360° Product View + What Fits Inside? + Product Comparison + Customer Reviews + Personalized Recommendations + International Localization.

The goal is to reduce uncertainty when purchasing premium handbags online and make the digital experience closer to visiting a physical Polène boutique.

Case Study Logic

Target Audience

Urban professionals, mainly women aged 25–40, looking for distinctive premium accessories.

Differentiation

Sculptural silhouettes, Parisian identity, recognizable design and a premium approach without relying on discounts.

Vendor Direct Fulfillment

Amazon handles the retail relationship while Polène keeps inventory and ships orders directly, supporting greater control over premium packaging and fulfillment.
