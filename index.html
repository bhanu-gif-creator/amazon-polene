The brand × marketplace concept

Luxury feeling. Thoughtful convenience.

Polène is known for its sculptural silhouettes, French design identity and leather craftsmanship. This student concept explores how a tightly controlled, brand-operated Amazon.fr seller storefront could add discoverability while preserving a premium customer experience.

Not a real partnership: the products, prices, imagery and cart are demonstration content only.

KEDGE · Amazon case study

The strategy behind the concept

An academic proposal, not an announcement of a real Amazon launch.

01 · Audience

Style-conscious professionals, primarily women aged 25–40 in French urban centres, seeking distinctive premium handbags.

02 · Differentiation

Sculptural shapes, understated aesthetics and a recognizable Parisian design identity distinguish the proposed assortment.

03 · Distribution

Propose a brand-operated third-party seller model to retain greater control over product content, presentation and assortment.

04 · Discovery

Curated search-friendly listings, premium photography and a brand storefront could support shoppers discovering the collection.

05 · Experience

Clear product information, easy comparison and transparent delivery details reduce friction without relying on discounts.

06 · Safeguards

Brand approval, authentic imagery, authorized distribution and consistent pricing would be prerequisites for any real launch.
